The Wall

The Wall is the social side of DNA Connect: a timeline where people post publicly under their name. Unlike your messages, the Wall is meant to be seen.

Posting

Write a post and it appears under your name in the timeline of everyone who has you as a contact or follows you. Others can like your post, comment on it, and reply to each other in threads.

Tips — appreciation with real weight

If you enjoy someone's post, you can send the author a tip in CPUNK right from the post. The tip is a real token transfer from your wallet to theirs — no platform in between, no cut taken by anyone (only the usual small network fee applies). During the current test phase, if you also hold DNAC the app mirrors the tip on DNA Chain as well — part of testing the chain with real activity.

Boosting

When writing a post you can boost it — once per day — to put it on the timeline of everyone, not just the people who follow you. Boosted posts are visibly marked.

Remember: the Wall is public

Think before you post

Your messages are private; the Wall is not. Posts are published under your name for others to see, and on a decentralized network you should assume a post, once seen, cannot be fully taken back. Post accordingly.
