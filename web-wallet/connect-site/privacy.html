Nodus Connect browser extension — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

This policy explains what the Nodus Connect browser extension for Google Chrome does with your information. Nodus Connect is a self-custody wallet with built-in Nodus Connect messaging. You do not create an account with us, and we do not collect analytics.

Questions about this policy: privacy@nodusnetwork.io

1. Summary

  • Your recovery phrase, password and private keys stay on your device. The extension never sends them anywhere.
  • To work, the extension sends only what each feature needs: your public addresses and the transactions you approve go to the blockchain networks you use, and your Nodus Connect messages go through the Nodus network.
  • Websites get your address or a signature only after you approve them.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. We do not sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your wallet. Your recovery phrase, encrypted with the password you choose.
  • Wallet records. Your transaction history, recent balances, address book and the list of websites you connected. These are stored encrypted and can be read only while your wallet is unlocked.
  • Nodus Connect. Your messages, contacts, profiles, groups and shared vaults, stored encrypted.
  • Settings. Your network and server choices, the auto-lock time and whether you agreed to the data notice. These are not encrypted. The status of transactions you just sent is also kept, without encryption, until the browser closes, so the extension can notify you.

Nothing is stored in Chrome sync, so nothing is copied to your other devices.

3. Information that leaves your device

Blockchain networks

To show your balances and history and to send the transactions you approve, the extension sends your public address for that network, and the signed transaction, to a public server of that network. While the wallet is unlocked, it also checks for incoming funds once a minute. If you enter your own server address for Ethereum or BNB Smart Chain, that server receives the same information instead.

Prices

To show prices, the extension asks DefiLlama for the prices of a fixed list of assets. Your addresses are not sent.

Nodus network

Your Nodus public key and address, your Nodus balance, history and name lookups, the Nodus transactions you approve, and your Nodus Connect messages, contact requests, groups and payments in chat go through the Nodus network's nodes. The profile name and picture you choose to publish are visible on the Nodus network.

Websites you visit

Websites can see that the wallet is installed, the extension's identifier and which network it uses, and can ask it to read public blockchain data. A website gets your address or a signature only after you approve it in the extension's own window. The extension does not read the content of the pages you visit.

Connection data

As with any internet connection, every server the extension contacts also sees your IP address. All connections are encrypted.

4. Service providers

The blockchain and price servers are run by independent providers under their own privacy policies:

  • Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain: PublicNode, dRPC, MEV Blocker (if you choose it), BNB Chain, Defibit, Ninicoin, and Blockscout for Ethereum history.
  • Solana: Solana Vibe Station; test networks: Solana Foundation.
  • TRON: TRON DAO.
  • Algorand: Nodely (AlgoNode).
  • Cellframe: Cellframe.
  • Splendor: Splendor.
  • Soqucoin: Soqucoin.
  • Prices: DefiLlama.
  • Ixios, Nervos CKB, QTC, XION and Asentum: the public servers of each network.

5. Notifications

The extension shows a notification when a transaction you sent is pending, confirmed or failed, and when funds arrive at your address. It sends no other notifications.

6. What we do not do

We do not use analytics, tracking or error-reporting services, show advertising, sell or rent data, or use data to decide creditworthiness or lending. Data leaves your device only as described in section 3.

The use of information described in this policy complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can disconnect any website at any time under "Connected sites".
  • "Delete saved wallet" removes your wallet, your wallet records and your Nodus Connect history from this device.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome removes the data it stored on this device.
  • Transactions on public blockchains are public and permanent. Messages and profile data already sent through the Nodus network cannot be recalled by deleting them from your device.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the extension handles data changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.
