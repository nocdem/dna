Nodus Connect browser extension — Privacy policy

Last updated: {DATE}

This policy covers the Nodus Connect extension for Google Chrome: the wallet and the Nodus Connect messages inside it.

Questions about this policy: privacy@nodusnetwork.io

1. In short

  • Your recovery phrase, your password and your private keys stay on your device. The extension does not send them to any server.
  • To show balances, send transactions and carry Nodus Connect messages, the extension contacts the public servers of the networks it supports and the Nodus network's nodes. They receive what each feature needs, listed below.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no account with us. We do not sell data.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Your wallet: the 24-word recovery phrase, encrypted with the password you choose (AES-256-GCM; the key is derived from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations, a new random salt per save).
  • Encrypted with a key from your unlocked wallet: your sent transactions, account history, last balances and address book.
  • Nodus Connect: your message history, contacts, profiles, groups and shared vaults, each record encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written; only a message counter is kept next to them without encryption.
  • Encrypted with a key from your unlocked wallet: the list of websites you connected and the account each one may see.
  • Stored without encryption: your network and server choices, the auto-lock time, the balance refresh interval, whether you agreed to the data notice shown when the extension is first opened, a message counter, and, until the browser closes, the status of transactions you sent (used for notifications).
  • Nothing is stored in Chrome's sync storage, so nothing is copied to other devices.

3. What leaves your device, and to whom

Each request goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS; for the Nodus network, WSS with the nodes' keys fixed in the extension). Every server also receives your IP address and normal connection information, as with any website.

a) Public blockchain servers

They receive your public address for the network concerned (to read balances and history) and the transactions you sign and choose to send. While the wallet is unlocked, the extension checks once a minute for incoming funds on Nodus, Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Solana, TRON, Cellframe and Ixios, so those servers see your address once a minute.

  • Ethereum: ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com (default), eth.drpc.org or rpc.mevblocker.io (your choice), or a server address you enter yourself; account history: eth.blockscout.com.
  • BNB Smart Chain: bsc-dataseed.binance.org (default), bsc-dataseed1.binance.org, bsc-dataseed2.binance.org, bsc-dataseed3.binance.org, bsc-dataseed4.binance.org, bsc-dataseed1.defibit.io, bsc-dataseed1.ninicoin.io, bsc-rpc.publicnode.com (your choice), or a server address you enter yourself.
  • Solana: public.rpc.solanavibestation.com; test networks you choose: api.testnet.solana.com, api.devnet.solana.com.
  • TRON: api.trongrid.io; test network you choose: nile.trongrid.io.
  • Cellframe: rpc.cellframe.net. Ixios: ixios-rpc.innova.limited.
  • Algorand: mainnet-api.algonode.cloud, mainnet-idx.algonode.cloud (test network: testnet-api.algonode.cloud, testnet-idx.algonode.cloud).
  • Nervos CKB: mainnet.ckb.dev (test network: testnet.ckb.dev). QTC: qtc.events (test network: testnet.qtc.events).
  • Soqucoin: mainnet-rpc.soqu.org, mainnet-api.soqu.org.
  • XION: api.xion-mainnet-1.burnt.com (test network: api.xion-testnet-2.burnt.com).
  • Splendor: quantum-rpc.splendor.org (test network: quantum-rpc-testnet.splendor.org). Asentum: testnet.asentum.com.

When a website you connected asks to read data from Ethereum or BNB Smart Chain, the extension passes that read request to the server you chose for that network.

b) Prices

coins.llama.fi receives a fixed list of asset names to return prices. It does not receive your addresses.

c) The Nodus network's nodes

164.68.116.180, 154.38.182.161, 161.97.85.25, 156.67.24.125, 156.67.25.251, 164.68.105.227. While the wallet is open, the extension connects to them with your Nodus public key and address. They receive your Nodus balance, history and name queries, the Nodus transactions you sign, and all Nodus Connect traffic: contact requests, messages, reactions, group messages, payments in chat, and the profile name and picture you choose to publish. Your profile name and picture are published to the Nodus network.

d) Websites

Any website you open can see that the wallet is installed, the extension's identifier and which network it is set to, and can ask it to read public blockchain data (passed to the server you chose). Your address, your Nodus address and public key, and any signature are given to a website only after you approve it in the extension's own window; each signature is approved one by one. The extension does not read the content of web pages.

4. Notifications

The extension shows a system notification when a transaction you sent is pending, confirmed or failed, and when funds arrive at your own address. It sends no other notifications.

5. What we do not do

No analytics or tracking scripts, no error-reporting service, no advertising, no sale or rental of data, no use of data to decide creditworthiness or lending. The extension sends data only to the servers, nodes and websites listed in section 3.

The use of the information described in this policy complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • "Delete saved wallet" in the extension removes the encrypted wallet, sent transactions, history, balances, address book, the list of connected websites and the Nodus Connect message history from this device.
  • "Connected sites" lets you disconnect any website at any time.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome removes the extension's local storage.
  • Transactions you send on the supported networks are public. Messages and profile data sent to the Nodus network have left your device; deleting your data from this device does not remove what was already sent.

7. Changes

When the extension's handling of data changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.
