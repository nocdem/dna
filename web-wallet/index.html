A MOMENT OF CARE

Before you use a web wallet.

Your recovery phrase controls your funds.
Keep it private, wherever you use your wallet.

  1. 01 /

    Keep your words to yourself.

    Never send your recovery phrase in a chat, email or support message. Anyone with those words can control your funds. Keep a private offline backup.

  2. 02 /

    Pause before you paste.

    Do not enter recovery words into a site linked in a message or an ad. Check the exact domain in your browser. Support will never need your phrase to “verify” or “sync” your wallet.

  3. 03 /

    Use a browser you trust.

    A web wallet depends on your device and browser. Avoid shared devices and untrusted extensions. Lock your wallet when you finish; keep your recovery backup offline.
