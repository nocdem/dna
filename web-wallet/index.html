OPTIONAL · SAVE ON THIS DEVICE

Encrypted in this browser.

Open your wallet, expand Save wallet on this device, set a unique local password, then select Save current wallet. The app encrypts your words before saving them to this site’s localStorage in your current browser profile.

The app does not upload or sync this copy, and does not store your password.

Protection depends on your password and browser.

A strong password helps protect a stolen encrypted copy. It cannot stop a fake page, malware, a malicious extension or compromised site code from capturing your words or password when you enter or unlock them. This web wallet is a development preview; tests and reviews are not a guarantee against theft.

How is the saved copy protected?

The wallet uses AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Its encryption key is derived from your password with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 and 600,000 iterations. Each save uses a new random salt and encryption nonce.

This makes password guessing more costly, but a weak or reused password can still expose the saved copy. Someone who copies it can try guesses offline, without a website login limit. Your password is used locally and is not saved by the wallet.

What does locking actually do?

Lock clears the app’s active wallet and input fields. The app also locks after 10 minutes of inactivity and when you leave the page. If you chose to save, the encrypted device copy remains available for a later unlock.

While the wallet is open, recovery words and signing keys are available in browser memory. JavaScript cannot guarantee complete memory erasure after locking. Locking also cannot revoke information already copied by an attacker.

What can erase my saved copy?

Clearing site data, deleting a browser profile, using private browsing or losing your device can remove the local copy. Another browser or device will not automatically have it. Browser or device backups may retain separate copies.

Keep all 24 words in their exact order in a private offline backup. Do not rely on a screenshot, email, chat or cloud note. If both your usable saved copy and recovery words are lost, we cannot recover the wallet. Deleting local data does not move on-chain funds.

Which password should I use, and what if I change it?

Use a unique password of at least 16 characters, preferably randomly generated by a trusted password manager or made from several randomly chosen words. Never use your recovery phrase as the password. The minimum length check alone does not prove a password is strong.

Changing the local password encrypts this saved copy again. It does not change your recovery phrase or blockchain keys, and does not invalidate a previously copied wallet file or leaked phrase. There is no password reset service; your offline recovery words are essential.
