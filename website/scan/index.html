Estimated APY at the current block pace
—

Rewards are paid every 17 280 blocks (24 epochs of 720). The payday estimate follows the current block pace; blocks are slower when the chain is idle, so the date can move later.

APY estimate: the reward reserve paid out at 1/65 536 per epoch, at the last 24 hours’ block pace, divided by the active validators’ stake — before validator commission; participation rules apply. Fees that refill the reserve are not counted.
