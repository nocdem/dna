Validators counts the bonded validators — those in the active set and those waiting to be selected; a validator that is leaving is not counted. Staked by validators is their own bonds; Delegated is every NODUS delegated to them, a validator’s delegation to itself included. Delegations counts each delegator–validator pair once: an address delegating to two validators counts twice. Read from a node’s current table.

Rewards

Paydays, the next one, and an APY estimate.

Next payday

Estimated APY at the current block pace
—

Rewards are paid every 17 280 blocks (24 epochs of 720). The payday estimate follows the current block pace; blocks are slower when the chain is idle, so the date can move later.

APY estimate: the reward reserve paid out at 1/65 536 per epoch, at the last 24 hours’ block pace, divided by the active validators’ stake — before validator commission; participation rules apply. Fees that refill the reserve are not counted.
