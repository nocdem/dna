Planned hard forks

Not on the chain yet. A planned fork has no date: it switches on at a block height only after every validator runs a release that knows it and the validators vote it in.

  • HF-5 — Smart contracts: Nodus EVM. Our own EVM engine runs Ethereum smart contracts under the Prague rules (blob and set-code transactions are not supported), with 32-byte addresses from post-quantum keys and a built-in bridge for NODUS. Built and tested; not released yet.
  • HF-6 — Lower validator stake, node roles and a power cap. A validator bond of at least 2,000,000 NODUS, storage / compute / bandwidth roles with their own stake, voting power capped from 10%, and the owner’s key separate from the node’s key. Designed; not built yet.
  • Storage role reward. Storage nodes earn from the Storage pool when validators confirm they serve stored data. Being built; it takes the next fork number when it is ready.
