AIRDROP / TESTNET

Airdrop.

Nodus reserves part of its fixed supply for airdrop campaigns. The first is CPUNK Community Airdrop; other airdrops will follow. This page explains the overall budget and how to take part in the first campaign.

Airdrop budget.

The total budget for all airdrops is 50,000,000 NODUS — 5% of the fixed 1,000,000,000 NODUS supply.

Airdrop budget
CampaignNODUSShare of total supply
CPUNK Community Airdrop (first)10,000,0001%
Future airdrops40,000,0004%
Total airdrop budget50,000,0005%

All percentages are shares of the total NODUS supply. The CPUNK campaign uses 1% of total supply, not 1% of the airdrop budget.

First airdrop: CPUNK community.

CPUNK Community Airdrop shares 10,000,000 NODUS among CPUNK holders. It is paid out every day for 365 days (one year), and each day you claim your share of the previous day. It starts soon; the exact start will be announced. The conditions below apply only to this first campaign.

Nodus Chain is currently a testnet. This page describes how the airdrop works. It makes no statement about price or value, and it is not an offer.

Which CPUNK counts.

Only CPUNK held at the CPUNK address of your own Nodus Connect wallet counts.

  • Your Nodus Connect CPUNK address. Nodus Connect derives this address, a Cellframe Backbone address, from your 24 recovery words.
  • Not on an exchange. CPUNK held on an exchange does not count.
  • No other wallet. No other wallet is linked to the airdrop.

Your share.

Your share follows how much CPUNK your address holds.

  • Whole units of 1,000,000 CPUNK. CPUNK is counted in whole units of 1,000,000. An address holding less than 1,000,000 CPUNK receives nothing.
  • The lowest balance of each day. Your address is counted at its lowest balance of each day.

Step by step.

  • 1. Create your Nodus Connect account. Open connect.nodusnetwork.io, create your account and write down your 24 recovery words.
  • 2. Find your CPUNK address. In Connect’s wallet, find your CPUNK address.
  • 3. Move your CPUNK there. Send your CPUNK to that address from wherever you hold it now.
  • 4. Register for the airdrop. A “Register for airdrop” button is coming to Nodus Connect. Registering is one click; nothing is moved and nothing is paid.
  • 5. Choose your name in Nodus Connect. After you register, a small amount of NODUS is sent to you to cover registering your on-chain name. This is only for an address holding at least 1,000,000 CPUNK.
  • 6. Claim every day. Open Connect and press Claim; a countdown shows how long you have. Each day you claim the previous day’s share. A share that is not claimed in time goes back to the airdrop pool and is shared out over the remaining days.

How it is checked.

  • Signed on your device. When you register, Connect signs on your device that the CPUNK address and your Nodus account belong together. Your recovery words never leave your device.
  • Checked by the airdrop service. The Nodus Foundation’s airdrop service checks the signatures and the balances and sends the NODUS.
  • Published every day. Each day’s list of counted addresses is published with its fingerprint (hash), so anyone can check the payouts.

Stay safe.

We never DM you and never ask for your recovery words. Only use the links on nodusnetwork.io.

More.

The CPUNK campaign uses 10,000,000 NODUS of the 50,000,000 NODUS airdrop budget. The remaining 40,000,000 NODUS is reserved for future airdrops. See Tokenomics for the full supply allocation.
