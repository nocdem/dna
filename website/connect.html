MADE FOR EVERYDAY CONNECTIONS

One identity.
So much you can do.

Messaging, social life, and a wallet live together inside Connect. These are features of the existing DNA Connect application, now becoming Nodus.

01

Keep the conversation going.

Private and group chats, delivery and read receipts, and an offline message queue of up to seven days.

02

Say it in your own way.

Voice messages, photos, and videos. A persistent media outbox keeps track of uploads and resumes them.

03

Share with your world.

A public wall, profiles, and follows give your community a place to meet. Public posts are visible to other people.

04

Pay the person in the chat.

Connect resolves your contact’s wallet address. Send and receive with QR codes, then find the transaction in your history.

05

Bring your networks together.

Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Solana, TRON, and Cellframe, plus native NODUS on testnet. Sending NODUS from Connect is not yet available on the current testnet.

06

Start with keys you control.

Create or restore your identity with a 24-word recovery phrase. A registered name connects it to the people you know.

NODUS CONNECT / PREVIEW

Now on the web, as a preview.

Nodus Connect is open on the web as a preview: your wallet and your messages in one app, at connect.nodusnetwork.io. The same 24-word recovery phrase opens your existing identity. The DNA Connect apps are no longer distributed.
