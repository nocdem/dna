Wallet

Your accounts, together inside Connect.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

One phrase, multiple networks

The wallet derives accounts for Cellframe, Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Solana and TRON, alongside NODUS on the native Nodus Chain testnet. These networks retain their own transaction rules and cryptography.

Send and receive

Select the network and asset, then choose a contact or enter the recipient address. QR codes support send and receive flows. Check the amount, destination, network and displayed fee before confirming. Transaction history shows your activity. On the current testnet, NODUS is sent from the web wallet and from Nodus Connect on the web (preview).

In the web wallet and Nodus Connect

The web wallet and Nodus Connect run in your browser and open with the same 24-word recovery phrase; both are previews. On the testnet they offer:

  • Send and receive NODUS, to an address or to a chain name.
  • An address book for every network the wallet sends on: NODUS, Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Solana and TRON. Each address is checked with that network’s own rules. The address book is stored encrypted only while a saved wallet is open; otherwise it lasts until you lock. The web wallet and Nodus Connect keep separate address books.
  • Earn. Delegate NODUS from a validator’s row in the validator list: at least 100 NODUS for a new delegation, any amount to add more, up to 2,048 delegators per validator. Withdrawn NODUS is locked for 12 epochs after it leaves the validator’s voting power. Becoming a validator is not in the wallet; validators are set up with the nodus-cli tool.
  • Shared vaults. A vault is a NODUS address with 2 to 7 members that needs M of them to approve a payment. Members are chosen by chain name or ID. Payments are proposed and approved in Nodus Connect, where members are told by message.
  • A chain name for your Nodus address; see Identity & profile.
  • A quick start in Nodus Connect. Connect opens at once from what this device keeps — contacts, conversations and names — and connects to the network in the background. Sending waits until it is connected.

Swaps

Swaps are not in the app today; the command-line tool keeps DEX quotes and swaps. A messaging security feature does not change the security of an external chain.

NODUS on testnet

Native balances currently belong to the testnet, and its coin is NODUS. Some code and tools still use the DNAC name during the transition.

A browser wallet is in development preview at wallet.nodusnetwork.io, and Nodus Connect is open as a preview at connect.nodusnetwork.io.
