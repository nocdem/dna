Wallet

Your accounts, together inside Connect.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

One phrase, multiple networks

The wallet derives accounts for Cellframe, Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Solana and TRON, alongside the native DNAC development chain. These networks retain their own transaction rules and cryptography.

Send and receive

Select the network and asset, then choose a contact or enter the recipient address. QR codes support send and receive flows. Check the amount, destination, network and displayed fee before confirming. Transaction history shows your activity.

Supported swaps

Integrated DEX routes offer swaps on supported networks. Available routes, fees and transaction protection depend on the network and provider. A messaging security feature does not change the security of an external chain.

DNAC today, NODUS at testnet

Native balances currently belong to the DNAC devnet. The network can reset. The Nodus name and NODUS ticker are planned for the testnet transition.
