The public wall

A place to share with your community.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

Posts are public

The Wall is the public side of Connect. Publish under your name, follow people, like posts and join comment threads. Unlike a private conversation, a public post is intended to be seen by others.

Choose what belongs in public

A reader can copy or redistribute a post. Do not treat deletion as a guarantee that every copy disappears. Use private conversations for content intended only for selected recipients.

Tips and existing assets

The existing app includes CPUNK tipping and devnet-related testing. Rebranding the website does not convert CPUNK into NODUS. Review the asset, network and fee shown by your installed app before approving a tip.
