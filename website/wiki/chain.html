Chain & NODUS

The ledger behind the network’s next chapter.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

A test network

The native chain has run as a public testnet since 30 September 2026, and its coin is NODUS. There are no more resets: changes to the chain’s rules take effect as hard forks at an agreed block height. Some code and tools still use the DNAC name during the transition.

Hard forks on the testnet

Four hard forks are active. The first was set in the genesis document; the other three were switched on by witness votes. A fifth, HF-5, has been voted in and is not active yet.

  • HF-1, from block 0: a transaction pays a fee of its units times the gas price, with a minimum.
  • HF-2, from block 1,500: governance approvals are weighed by voting power and need more than two thirds of it.
  • HF-3, from block 2,926: a block is bounded only by the consensus limits.
  • HF-4, from block 3,151: rule-set generation 2, with on-chain names.
  • HF-5, voted, switches on at block 79,757: rule-set generation 3, with smart contracts on Nodus EVM. Voted in at block 62,425.

What is implemented

Development includes transparent UTXO accounting, native assets, staking, delegation and chain parameters. Ledger V2 adds modular domains and independent genesis. The CometBFT reference port is merged and runs the testnet, together with its peer-to-peer layer on port 4004. Scan reads this chain.

These implementation milestones do not announce a deployed public service. Shielded payments remain development work.

Earn NODUS

On the testnet today there are two ways to earn NODUS, both paid from the 200,000,000 NODUS reward reserve: run a witness, or delegate NODUS to one.

  • Run a witness: bond exactly 10,000,000 NODUS of your own and run a node that signs blocks. Up to 32 witnesses are active, chosen by total stake. Each witness sets its commission, up to 50%. Witnesses are set up with the nodus-cli tool. After unstaking, the bond is locked for 84 epochs.
  • Delegate: at least 100 NODUS, from Earn in the web wallet or Nodus Connect. Undelegated NODUS is locked for 12 epochs.

An epoch is 720 blocks. At the end of each epoch, 1/65,536 of the reserve is shared by stake among the witnesses that met the participation rule, and their delegators get their part minus the witness’s commission. Rewards are paid every 24 epochs, on a payday every 17,280 blocks. No stake is cut today.

Planned, not live

The changes below have been decided but are not running on the chain. They arrive only with a hard fork that witnesses vote in, and the numbers can still change. HF-6 brings the first four items, HF-7 the storage role reward and HF-8 the chain-name rule for delegating. HF-5, Nodus EVM, is no longer planned: witnesses voted it in at block 62,425 and it switches on at block 79,757. Our own EVM engine runs Ethereum smart contracts under the Prague rules (blob and set-code transactions are not supported), with 32-byte addresses from post-quantum keys and a built-in bridge for NODUS.

  • HF-6: the witness bond drops from exactly 10,000,000 to a minimum of 2,000,000 NODUS; stake above the bond counts as delegation to yourself.
  • Separate roles with their own stake: storage 1,000,000, compute 1,000,000 and bandwidth 500,000 NODUS. These amounts become chain parameters that can be changed by vote.
  • The voting power counted for one witness is capped, starting at 10% of total power and changeable by vote.
  • The owner’s key, which puts up the stake and can unstake, becomes separate from the node’s key on the server.
  • HF-7: a storage role reward: nodes that register in the storage role with a 1,000,000 NODUS stake earn from the Storage pool for keeping their assigned parts of the block archive, in proportion to the blocks they keep. Witnesses do not earn storage rewards; a storage node and a witness can be the same machine. It is built and runs on the testnet nodes, switched off, until witnesses vote it in.
  • HF-8: a new delegation, or adding to one, needs the delegating address to own a chain name. Existing delegations and their rewards are not affected, and a witness delegating to itself does not need a name.
