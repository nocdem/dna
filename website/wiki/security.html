Privacy & security

Understand what is protected, and what you choose to make public.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

Private content

Private message bodies are end-to-end encrypted. Local application databases use SQLCipher encryption. Public profiles and wall posts are intentionally discoverable; they are not private messages.

Network visibility

Nodes accepting connections can observe IP addresses, timing, traffic volume and routing metadata. Encryption of message content does not provide anonymity. TCP channel protection does not extend to all UDP discovery traffic.

Protect your device

Use the app’s PIN lock and supported biometrics alongside your device screen lock. Keep your recovery phrase private. Encryption cannot stop someone controlling an unlocked device or a recipient who shares a conversation.

Cryptography and review

The current private-message implementation uses Kyber1024 round-3 and AES-256-GCM, with the Dilithium5 / ML-DSA-87 signature profile. Kyber round-3 predates ML-KEM/FIPS 203. These choices do not constitute an independent audit or certification.

The native libraries are Apache 2.0; the Flutter app has a separate source-available proprietary license.
