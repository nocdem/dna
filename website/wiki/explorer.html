Using Nodus Scan

Follow a block. Find a transaction. Understand an address.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

Find a record

Search by decimal block height, a transaction position written as height:index, a block id, a transaction wire id or intent id, or an address fingerprint; searching for a chain name finds its registration. Ids and address fingerprints use 128 hexadecimal characters. If a term matches more than one record type, choose the result you need.

Read the detail pages

Block pages show the header and the block’s transactions, page by page. Payday blocks also list recipient addresses and reward amounts, with their own page control. Transaction pages show the position, status (applied or refused), wire and intent ids, fee, inputs and outputs, and any recorded staking or configuration change; a chain-name registration also shows the registered name and the price paid into the reward reserve. Address pages show balances per token as reported by the network — total, spendable now and coin count — and the address’s indexed transaction history. A separate rewards section shows recorded payouts, pending rewards and dated payout history. Recorded payouts cover the displayed period, not lifetime earnings; paid rewards are already part of the balance while unspent. The coverage label names the first recorded block and the source block for pending rewards.

Throughput and paydays

The Scan home page provides search and the latest blocks. The Statistics page shows, besides the supply figures:

  • Throughput — transactions per second over the last minute and the last hour, and a chart of the last 24 hours by UTC hour. Only applied transactions count, measured by block time up to the newest indexed block.
  • Next payday — rewards are paid at every block whose height is a multiple of 17,280. Scan estimates its date at the current block pace; the date moves later while the chain is idle.
  • Paydays — past paydays with their block, time, total paid and recipient count. Load older paydays independently of the block list. Open a payday block for individual payouts, then an address for its payout history and pending rewards. Payouts happen at the block boundary and are listed separately from transactions. Unavailable historical records are labelled as unavailable.
  • Estimated APY at the current block pace — an estimate before witness commission; participation rules apply and fees are not counted.

Hard forks page

The Hard forks page lists each hard fork with its rule in one sentence, the block it was voted in, the block it takes effect at and whether it is active or still pending. Other governance changes are listed below it.

Understand the data source

Scan is a read-only indexer that trusts responses from its configured witness cluster. Indexed data can lag; balances come live from the network and may reflect a later block than the indexed history. An unavailable API is shown as unavailable, never as an empty chain or zero balance.

Current supply is the supply reported by the testnet. It is not a claim about market circulation.
