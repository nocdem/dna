Messaging

Private conversations, from a quick hello to a shared video.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

Private by default

Private and group message content is end-to-end encrypted. Encryption protects content between participants; it does not stop a recipient from copying a message or protect a device someone else controls.

More than text

Send voice messages, photos and videos. The media outbox tracks uploads and supports resuming them. Message reactions, starred messages, delivery receipts and read receipts help you follow a conversation.

When a contact is offline

The offline queue uses a default retention window of up to seven days for encrypted messages. Delivery depends on the recipient returning and the network retaining the data. This queue is separate from your long-term message history.

Keep your history

Local conversation history is stored in encrypted application databases. Optional encrypted message backup is available in Settings → Data & Storage. A recovery phrase recreates keys; it cannot recreate a message that no longer exists in any available backup or synchronized storage.
