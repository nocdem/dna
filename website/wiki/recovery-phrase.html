Recovery & backups

Keep your keys safe. Know what a backup brings back.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

Your 24 words

The recovery phrase recreates your identity keys and derived wallet accounts. Save the words in their exact order, keep copies in secure places and never share them. Enter them only in the official app’s recovery flow.

No website, wiki, explorer or support request needs your recovery phrase.

Keys and history are different

A recovery phrase is not a copy of your message history or files. Enable encrypted message backup in Settings → Data & Storage if you want that recovery option. Recovery still depends on the retained backup being available.

Restore on another device

  1. Install the official Connect release.
  2. Choose restore and enter the phrase in order.
  3. Allow available identity and contact records to synchronize.
  4. Restore an available encrypted message backup when offered.

Without the keys or phrase, support cannot reconstruct your identity for you.
