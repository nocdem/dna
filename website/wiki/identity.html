Identity & profile

A name people can find. Keys you keep.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

Your name and keys

Your identity starts with keys created on your device. A registered name connects those keys to a readable handle, so contacts can find you without exchanging phone numbers.

Chain names

Since block 3,151 of the testnet (the HF-4 hard fork), a name can also be registered on the chain itself. Register one for your Nodus address with “Register a name” in the web wallet or in Nodus Connect. People can then send NODUS to your name instead of your long address, and Nodus Connect shows each contact by one name — the chain name, or else the profile name — with the ID underneath.

  • 3 to 36 characters: English letters a–z and digits 0–9 only. The wallet turns capital letters into lowercase; dots, dashes and other characters are not allowed.
  • A name made only of the characters 0–9 and a–f that is 8 or more characters long is refused, because it would look like an ID.
  • One name per address, first come first served. A name is permanent: it does not expire, and it cannot be transferred today.
  • Today’s price is 1,000 NODUS for 3 characters, 500 for 4, 100 for 5 and 1 for 6 or more, plus the network fee. The price goes to the reward reserve, and validators can change it by vote.
A registration that is waiting to enter a block can be seen by others. If the same name is registered in an earlier block, yours is refused and its price is not charged.

A name registered in the DNA Connect app is a separate record; Nodus Connect shows it as a name only after checking that it belongs to that identity. Scan finds a chain-name registration when you search for the name.

Choose what you share

Your avatar, bio, location and website are optional profile details. Published names, profiles and wallet addresses are discoverable. Signed records help detect tampering; signing does not make a record private.

Payments by contact

Connect can look up a contact’s published wallet address for the chosen network. Review the recipient and network before confirming a transfer. Your private keys stay on your device.

Identity approvals

Supported services can request an identity approval through a QR login. Review the request in the app and approve only a login you started. Availability depends on the service and device.
