Getting started

Your first conversation starts with an identity you control.

REVIEWED SEPTEMBER 2026EN / TR

01. Get Connect

Connect brings private messaging, a public wall and a wallet together. Current Android, Linux and Windows downloads still use the DNA Connect name during the Nodus transition.

Choose your download →

02. Create your identity

Choose to create an identity in the app. Save the 24-word recovery phrase in order, then follow the name-registration steps. You do not need a phone number or email address.

Keep the phrase private. It controls your identity and derived wallet accounts. This wiki never asks you to enter it.

03. Find your people

Add a contact by their registered name. Private and group message content is encrypted before leaving your device. Voice messages, images and videos are supported; a voice message is different from a live call.

Explore messaging →

Already have an identity?

Choose restore and enter your recovery phrase in the official app. It restores your keys and derived accounts. Recovering old messages depends on available synchronized data and any encrypted backup you enabled.
