THE NODUS MANIFESTO

Privacy is a
human right.

Your identity. Your conversations. Your data.
The choice should always be yours.

Everyone deserves a private space to speak, connect, and create, wherever they live and whoever they are. We believe people should hold the keys to their digital identity and decide what they share, with whom, and for how long.

Why we are building Nodus

That belief started with a messenger. As we built it, the need grew: identity, storage, transport, and payments all needed a foundation we could shape. Building our own network and chain gives us responsibility for their architecture, security, and direction. Post-quantum cryptography is part of that foundation, because private conversations can matter long after they happen.

Nodus carries this work forward as shared infrastructure. Storage is the starting point, with Bandwidth and Compute planned next. Our ambition is a network where people can contribute resources and access the capacity they need: space for files, bandwidth for calls, or compute for proofs and AI training. The applications are how people use it. Independence is why we build it.

An identity you control.Data you choose to share.Infrastructure we build together.
