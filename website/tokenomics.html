The NODUS supply.

Nodus Chain is a testnet. Its coin, NODUS, has a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000, written into the chain’s genesis; no new coins are minted. This page shows how that supply is allocated and how validator rewards are paid. For the current figures, see Scan.

A fixed supply.

The total supply is 1,000,000,000 NODUS. The whole amount exists from the first block, divided among the allocations below. No new NODUS is minted: validator rewards are paid from a reserve that is part of the same fixed supply.

Nodus Chain is currently a testnet. This page describes the allocation the testnet genesis holds. It makes no statement about price or value, and it is not an offer.

How the genesis holds it.

The testnet genesis places each allocation in one of five forms.

  • Reward reserve — 200,000,000. Held by the chain itself and paid out to validators epoch by epoch, as described below.
  • Service pools — 300,000,000. Storage (100,000,000), Compute (100,000,000), Bandwidth (50,000,000) and Future services (50,000,000) are locked in chain state. No key controls them, and no rule that releases them exists yet.
  • Foundation multisig — 380,000,000. Security / bug bounty (50,000,000), Liquidity (150,000,000), Ecosystem / developer grants (100,000,000), Foundation (30,000,000) and Community airdrop (50,000,000) exist as coins from the first block, owned by the Foundation’s 2-of-3 multisig address: any spend needs two of its three keys.
  • Genesis validator stakes — 70,000,000. The chain starts with seven validators, each staking 10,000,000. These stakes come from the Foundation allocation, which is why the Foundation’s own coins are 30,000,000 of its 100,000,000. When a genesis validator retires, its stake returns to the Foundation multisig address.
  • Founder — 50,000,000. A genesis allocation that only the founder’s key can claim.

Validator rewards.

Validators are paid from the 200,000,000 reward reserve, not from new coins.

  • An epoch is 720 blocks. At the end of every epoch, 1/65,536 of the reserve (rounded down) is shared among the validators that governed the epoch, in proportion to their stake: their own stake plus what is delegated to them.
  • From the part earned by delegated stake, the validator takes its commission, and the rest goes to its delegators in proportion to their delegation. Each validator sets its own commission, up to 50%.
  • A validator that did not take part enough in an epoch earns nothing for it, and neither do its delegators; that share stays in the reserve.
  • Rewards are recorded every epoch and paid out every 24 epochs.
  • Transaction fees, including the fee for creating a token, are credited to the reward reserve. They are not burned.

Current figures.

The numbers above are the genesis allocation. Scan shows where the supply is now: open the Scan home page and choose Details on the Circulating supply card to see the reward reserve left, each service pool and the unclaimed allocation.

Circulating supply is every existing coin outside the reward reserve, the service pools and the unclaimed allocation. Staked coins and the Foundation’s coins count as circulating. On the first block this was 450,000,000 NODUS: the Foundation’s 380,000,000 and the 70,000,000 of validator stakes.
