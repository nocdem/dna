TOKENOMICS / TESTNET

The NODUS supply.

Nodus Chain is a testnet. Its coin, NODUS, has a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000, written into the chain’s genesis; no new coins are minted. This page shows how that supply is allocated and how witness rewards are paid. For the current figures, see Scan.

A fixed supply.

The total supply is 1,000,000,000 NODUS. The whole amount exists from the first block, divided among the allocations below. No new NODUS is minted: witness rewards are paid from a reserve that is part of the same fixed supply.

Nodus Chain is currently a testnet. This page describes the allocation the testnet genesis holds. It makes no statement about price or value, and it is not an offer.

How the genesis holds it.

The testnet genesis places each allocation in one of five forms.

  • Reward reserve — 200,000,000. Held by the chain itself and paid out to witnesses epoch by epoch, as described below.
  • Service pools — 300,000,000. Storage (100,000,000), Compute (100,000,000), Bandwidth (50,000,000) and Future services (50,000,000) are locked in chain state. No key controls them, and no rule that releases them exists yet.
  • Foundation multisig — 380,000,000. Security / bug bounty (50,000,000), Liquidity (150,000,000), Ecosystem / developer grants (100,000,000), Foundation (30,000,000) and Airdrops (50,000,000) exist as coins from the first block, owned by the Foundation’s 2-of-3 multisig address: any spend needs two of its three keys.
  • Genesis witness stakes — 70,000,000. The chain starts with seven witnesses, each staking 10,000,000. These stakes come from the Foundation allocation, which is why the Foundation’s own coins are 30,000,000 of its 100,000,000. When a genesis witness retires, its stake returns to the Foundation multisig address.
  • Founder — 50,000,000. A genesis allocation that only the founder’s key can claim.

Witness rewards.

Witnesses are paid from the 200,000,000 reward reserve, not from new coins.

  • An epoch is 720 blocks. At the end of every epoch, 1/65,536 of the reserve (rounded down) is shared among the witnesses that governed the epoch, in proportion to their stake: their own stake plus what is delegated to them.
  • From the part earned by delegated stake, the witness takes its commission, and the rest goes to its delegators in proportion to their delegation. Each witness sets its own commission, up to 50%.
  • A witness that did not take part enough in an epoch earns nothing for it, and neither do its delegators; that share stays in the reserve.
  • Rewards are recorded every epoch and paid out every 24 epochs.
  • Transaction fees, including the fee for creating a token, are credited to the reward reserve. They are not burned.

How to earn NODUS.

On the testnet today there are two ways to earn NODUS, and both are paid from the reward reserve: run a witness, or delegate NODUS to a witness. The sections below explain what each one needs, how a reward is calculated and when it is paid. Everything here describes the rules the chain runs today, unless a section says it is planned.

  • Run a witness. You bond your own NODUS, run a node that signs blocks, and earn a share of every epoch reward for your stake, plus a commission from your delegators.
  • Delegate. You bond NODUS to a witness someone else runs. You earn a share of that witness’s reward, minus its commission. You do not run a node.

The reserve left and the other live figures are on Scan; see Current figures below.

Epochs and paydays.

Rewards are counted in epochs and paid on paydays.

  • An epoch is 720 blocks. Blocks come about every 4–5 seconds while there are transactions and every 60 seconds when the chain is idle. An epoch therefore takes roughly one hour when the chain is busy and up to about twelve hours when it is idle.
  • The reward per epoch is the reward reserve divided by 65,536, rounded down. With the full 200,000,000 reserve that is about 3,051 NODUS per epoch. It shrinks slowly as the reserve is paid out, and grows again with fees, because every transaction fee is credited to the reserve.
  • Payday is every 24 epochs. Rewards are calculated at the end of every epoch and paid out every 24 epochs, as ordinary coins to the earner’s address.
  • Leaving does not erase rewards. If you unstake or undelegate, the rewards you have already earned are still paid on the normal payday.

How a reward is calculated.

At the end of each epoch the chain works out every share in three steps.

  • 1. Between witnesses. The epoch reward is shared among the witnesses that governed the epoch, in proportion to their stake: each witness’s own bond plus the delegations to it.
  • 2. Inside a witness’s share. The witness keeps the part earned by its own stake. From the part earned by delegated stake it takes its commission.
  • 3. To delegators. The rest of the delegated part goes to the witness’s delegators, in proportion to their delegation.

Three rules decide whether stake earns at all.

  • Whole epochs only. Only stake that is bonded through a whole epoch earns for that epoch.
  • Participation. In an epoch, a witness must sign at least 50% of the blocks and sign at least once in the epoch’s last 120 blocks. A witness that misses this earns nothing for the epoch, and its delegators earn nothing from it either. A witness that misses it in two consecutive epochs is removed from the active set.
  • What is not earned stays in the reserve. Shares that nobody earned and rounding remainders are not paid out; they stay in the reward reserve.

An example, not a forecast. Seven witnesses with equal stake share an epoch reward of about 3,051 NODUS, so each gets about 436 NODUS for the epoch. A delegator who holds 1% of one witness’s total stake, where that witness charges 10% commission, gets 1% × 436 × 90% ≈ 3.9 NODUS for the epoch — and only if the witness met the participation rule. Real figures depend on the reserve, the stakes and the commissions at the time.

No slashing. Today no stake, a witness’s or a delegator’s, is ever cut. Missing the participation rule costs that epoch’s reward, not stake.

Running a witness.

A witness runs a node that signs blocks and stores the network’s data.

  • Bond exactly 10,000,000 NODUS of your own to become a witness.
  • Up to 32 witnesses are active. They are chosen by total stake (own bond plus delegations), using the balances frozen at the previous epoch boundary. A new witness waits two full epochs before it can be chosen. Witnesses beyond the 32 wait as eligible candidates, with no signing duty and no reward, and are ranked again every epoch.
  • Commission is set by the witness, from 0% to 50%. An increase takes effect only after at least two epochs, so delegators can leave first; a decrease takes effect immediately.
  • Leaving. After unstaking, a witness stays in the set until the frozen list it is part of expires, and it must keep its node running and signing until then. Its bond is then locked for 84 epochs before it can be spent.
  • Tools. Today witnesses are set up with the command-line tool, nodus-cli. The web wallet’s button for becoming a witness was removed; it will return with the planned change described under Planned changes.
  • At launch the chain has seven Foundation witnesses, each with 10,000,000.

Delegating.

Delegating lets you earn without running a node.

  • Minimum 100 NODUS. A witness can have up to 2,048 delegators.
  • From the web wallet or Nodus Connect. Delegating is done under Earn, in the web wallet or in Nodus Connect.
  • What you earn follows the calculation above: your part of the witness’s delegated share, minus its commission, for every whole epoch in which the witness met the participation rule.
  • Undelegating. Your stake stays bonded until it leaves the witness’s voting power, and is then locked for 12 epochs. Moving to another witness counts as undelegating.

Planned changes.

Planned, not live. The changes below have been decided but are not running on the chain. They arrive only with a hard fork that is voted in, and the numbers can still change.

  • Lower witness bond. The witness bond drops to a minimum of 2,000,000 NODUS. Anything you stake above the bond counts as delegation to yourself, with the same 12-epoch lock as any delegation.
  • Separate roles with their own stake. Storage 1,000,000, compute 1,000,000, bandwidth 500,000 and witness 2,000,000. These amounts become chain parameters that can be changed by vote.
  • Power cap. The power counted for one witness is capped, starting at 10% of total power and changeable by vote. Stake above the cap does not count and earns nothing; the part up to the cap earns normally.
  • Separate owner and node keys. The owner’s key — the wallet that puts up the money and can unstake — becomes separate from the node’s key on the server. You can then run a node and stake to it from the web wallet, and an owner can be a multisig.
  • Storage rewards. Nodes that register in the storage role with a 1,000,000 NODUS stake earn an equal share from the Storage pool when witnesses confirm that they serve stored data. Witnesses do not earn storage rewards; a storage node and a witness can be the same machine. The design was approved on 4 October 2026 and is being built; it is switched on later by a hard fork that witnesses vote in.

Current figures.

The numbers above are the genesis allocation. Open Scan Statistics and choose Details below Total supply / Circulating supply to see the reward reserve left and each service pool.

Circulating supply is every existing coin outside the reward reserve, the service pools and the unclaimed allocation. Staked coins and the Foundation’s coins count as circulating. On the first block this was 450,000,000 NODUS: the Foundation’s 380,000,000 and the 70,000,000 of witness stakes.
